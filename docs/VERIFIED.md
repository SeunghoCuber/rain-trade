# Verified Venue Facts (Phase 0)

Answers to the "verify before building" questions in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) §3, plus other venue facts the harness depends on. Every claim cites a raw sample in [samples/](samples/) or an official doc. Verified on **2026-09-24** against BTC 15-minute markets. Re-check anything marked ⚠️ before relying on it.

Config values that come from this document live in [config/default.yaml](../config/default.yaml).

## Summary

| Question | Answer | Config |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Resolution source | Chainlink **BTC/USD 60 s TWAP** data stream. Up wins if close TWAP **≥** open TWAP. | `settlement.tieGoesTo: up`, `settlement.twapWindow` |
| (a) S₀ timing | Gamma publishes `priceToBeat` about 20 min after the boundary, i.e. after the market has closed. We compute S₀ ourselves: the mean of Chainlink 1 s ticks over [B−62 s, B−3 s]. | `fairValue.spotInput` |
| (b) Complementary liquidity | **Yes.** The book is unified: UP bid at p ≡ DOWN ask at 1−p, same size. Trades print once, on the taker's token. | `venue.complementaryMatching: true` |
| (c) Taker fee | `fee = C × 0.07 × p(1−p)`, rounded to 5 dp. Peak 1.75¢/share at p = 0.5. Makers pay 0. | `venue.feeSchedule` |
| (c) Maker rebate | 20% of the market's taker fees, split daily by maker fee-equivalent. | `venue.feeSchedule.rebateRate` |
| Tick / min size | 0.01 / 5 shares | `venue.tickSize`, `venue.minOrderSize` |

## 1. Market discovery

Source: [samples/gamma-event-current.json](samples/gamma-event-current.json), [samples/gamma-event-resolved.json](samples/gamma-event-resolved.json).

- **Slug is deterministic:** `btc-updown-15m-<windowStartUnixSec>`, with the window start aligned to 900 s. Fetch with `GET https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?slug=<slug>`. No search or polling of listings is needed.
- Markets are **created about 24 h ahead**. `startDate` is the creation time. The real window is `startTime` (event) or `eventStartTime` (market) to `endDate`. Don't use `startDate` as the window start.
- `markets[0].clobTokenIds` is a JSON-encoded string `[upTokenId, downTokenId]`, in the same order as `outcomes` `["Up","Down"]`.
- `conditionId` is the CLOB `market` id used on the WebSocket.
- Series: `btc-up-or-down-15m`. `cryptoMarketConfig = { id: "btc-15m-twap-60", twapEnabled: true, twapLookbackSeconds: 60 }`.
- `restricted: true`. This doesn't matter for read-only data access.

## 2. Resolution: question (a)

Market description (verbatim):

> This market will resolve to "Up" if the time-weighted average price (TWAP) of Bitcoin, generated by Chainlink, of the time range specified in the title is greater than or equal to the price at the beginning of that range. Otherwise, it will resolve to "Down". The resolution source for this market is information from Chainlink, specifically the BTC/USD TWAP data stream available at https://data.chain.link/streams/btc-usd-twap-60s-streams.

- **Tie rule:** `≥` goes to **Up**.
- **Official numbers:** after resolution, the Gamma event carries `eventMetadata: { priceToBeat, finalPrice }`, and the market's `outcomePrices` become `["1","0"]` (Up) or `["0","1"]` (Down). `umaResolutionStatus: "resolved"` and `automaticallyResolved: true`. `closedTime` is about 53–90 s after `endDate`.
- **Boundaries chain exactly:** `priceToBeat(k+1) == finalPrice(k)` for all 7 consecutive pairs checked (19:00–20:45 UTC). Each boundary therefore has one reference price, the TWAP evaluated at the boundary instant.
- ⚠️ **S₀ is published late**, about 20 min after the boundary (§2.1). The fair value engine can't wait for Gamma. It must compute S₀ from recorded Chainlink ticks and treat Gamma as a later cross-check (`SettlementMismatch`).
- ⚠️ **Don't use `polymarket.com/api/crypto/crypto-price`** (the website's "price to beat" endpoint). Its `openPrice` for the 21:15 window was `84320.13089763469`, exactly the **raw** Chainlink tick at 21:15:00.000, not the TWAP. For the 21:00 window it returned 84349.41, while the official Gamma `priceToBeat` was 84341.75.
- **TWAP window convention:** see §2.1.

### 2.1 Chainlink price feed

Source: [samples/rtds.ndjson](samples/rtds.ndjson), [samples/boundary-feeds.ndjson.gz](samples/boundary-feeds.ndjson.gz).

- Polymarket RTDS `wss://ws-live-data.polymarket.com` relays Chainlink prices. Subscribe with `{"action":"subscribe","subscriptions":[{"topic":"crypto_prices_chainlink","type":"*","filters":"{\"symbol\":\"btc/usd\"}"}]}` and send `PING` every 5 s.
- Payload: `{ symbol: "btc/usd", timestamp: <ms, whole second>, value, full_accuracy_value: "<18-decimal integer>" }`. Record `full_accuracy_value` as the lossless value.
- Cadence is **1 tick per second** (median spacing 1000 ms, max 2000 ms). Ticks arrive with a median **1.46 s delay** after their `timestamp`. Fair value logic must allow for this delay.
- **On every (re)subscribe, RTDS replays about 59 s of history** as one message: `{ topic: "crypto_prices", type: "subscribe", payload: { symbol, data: [{ timestamp, value }] } }`. It has no `full_accuracy_value`. The recorder stores these ticks as `res_price` with `backfill: true`, so a short outage loses no TWAP inputs.
- ⚠️ **RTDS skips seconds upstream.** Live recording misses about 1% of seconds: isolated seconds every 1–2 min, plus a 5–7 s stall about every 45 min (18 overnight). The next resubscribe's backfill has the **same holes**, so the recorder captured everything RTDS had.
- ⚠️ **RTDS reproduces the official TWAP only approximately** (measured 2026-09-25 over 26 boundaries, 8 h of recording):
  - **Full windows:** with all 60 ticks present, 9 of 12 boundaries matched exactly and 3 were off by up to $0.21. So RTDS sometimes relays slightly different values from the stream Polymarket settles on, not just fewer of them.
  - **Windows with missing ticks:** no fill rule is exact. Linear interpolation is best (median |Δ| $0.04, max $0.55). Averaging only the ticks present gives a median of $0.05 and a max of $0.70. In 3 of 8 single-gap cases the implied missing value was exactly the midpoint of its neighbors, but not in the rest.
  - **Overall:** our TWAP is within **≤ $0.70, about 0.08 bp**, of the official one. That's far below a 1¢ quote spread. Our outcome matched the official one in all 22 resolved markets.
  - **Design consequence:** settle PnL on the **official Gamma resolution** (Phase 6), and use our TWAP only as the real-time S₀ estimate for fair value (Phase 4). Health flags `TWAPΔ` above `settlement.twapToleranceUsd` ($1) and `SETTLE` when our outcome disagrees; `SETTLE` excludes the market.
- RTDS carries only spot symbols (btc, eth, sol, xrp, doge, bnb, hype, zec). No TWAP stream is exposed, so **we compute the 60 s TWAP ourselves** from the 1 s ticks.
- **TWAP window convention (matched exactly on 2 boundaries: 21:15 and 22:00 UTC on 2026-09-24; the second was checked end to end by `pnpm recorder:stats` on live recorded data):** the reference price at boundary B is the **simple mean of the 60 one-second Chainlink ticks with `timestamp` in [B − 62 s, B − 3 s]**. For the 21:15:00 UTC boundary this gives `84326.699345314844`, and the official `finalPrice(21:00)` = `priceToBeat(21:15)` = `84326.69934531485` (|Δ| = 6e-12). The next-best of 242 candidate windows was 0.006 away. The naive `[B − 60 s, B]` is about $0.28 off. Implemented as `chainlinkTwap()` in `packages/pm-harness-core/src/twap.ts`, tested against this boundary.
- **Publication delay:** that value appeared on Gamma at 21:35:04, about 20 min after the boundary and 5 min after the 21:15 market had already closed. S₀ is effectively **never** available from Gamma while a market is trading.

## 3. Order book and trades: question (b)

Source: [samples/clob-ws-market.ndjson.gz](samples/clob-ws-market.ndjson.gz) (75 s of one market, both tokens), [samples/clob-book-up.json](samples/clob-book-up.json), [samples/clob-book-down.json](samples/clob-book-down.json).

- Subscribe to `wss://ws-subscriptions-clob.polymarket.com/ws/market` with `{"assets_ids":[up,down],"type":"market"}` and send `PING` every 10 s.
- Message types seen: `book` (full snapshot for one token), `price_change` (level updates), `last_trade_price` (trade prints). Messages may arrive as a JSON array.
- **The book is unified.** All **31,644** `price_change` messages contained exactly two changes: `UP side@p` and `DOWN oppositeSide@(1−p)`, with identical size. In the final snapshots, UP bids and DOWN asks matched at **46/46** levels.
  - The REST `/book` snapshots differ slightly between tokens only because they were fetched about 170 ms apart.
  - **Implication:** a resting UP bid at p is filled by UP `SELL` prints at ≤ p **and** DOWN `BUY` prints at ≥ 1−p. `complementaryMatching` must be **on**, otherwise about half of the flow is missed.
- `price_change.size` is the **new total size** at the level (0 = removed), not an increment. Each change also carries `best_bid` / `best_ask`.
- **Trades print once**, on one token only, and `side` is the **taker's side**. For example, DOWN `BUY @0.70` lifts the DOWN ask, which is the same as hitting the UP bid at 0.30. Sizes can be fractional (e.g. `109.375`) because of dollar-denominated market orders. The print carries `fee_rate_bps` and `transaction_hash`.
- **Liquidity consumed by a match is never sent as a level update.** When an incoming order crosses, the feed sends the new resting remainder (if any) and a trade print, but no update removing the maker level it consumed. The consumption shows up only as a worse `best_bid`/`best_ask` on the next change, and in the next snapshot. The book model prunes every level better than the reported best, since matching takes the best levels first (`Book.pruneToReported`). Over 9 h this removed 4,737 levels.
- **An empty side is reported as `best_bid: "0"` / `best_ask: "1"`**, not as missing. This happens near resolution, when one side of the book empties.
- **Some resting orders disappear without a level update.** Before a snapshot, the rebuilt book is sometimes *larger* than the snapshot at a level, by round amounts (5, 15, 30, 40 shares), with no trade nearby. It happens mostly deep in the book, and in the first minute of a window. A likely cause is expiring (GTD) orders, or orders removed for insufficient balance. The next snapshot corrects it.
- **Snapshots always come in UP/DOWN pairs** with the same timestamp (25 + 25 in 75 s), so storing only the UP one loses nothing.
- **Reconstruction accuracy** (Phase 3, 10M events over 9 h, `pnpm replay`):
  - **Best bid/ask:** matches the venue's reported best on 99.9995% of 7.4M level changes.
  - **Snapshots:** 2.5% of 24.5k snapshots differ at some level, but only 112 levels in total are within 5 ticks of best, about 80% of them in a window's first minute.
  - **The Phase 0 capture rebuilds exactly**, with zero mismatches (`reconstruction.test.ts`).
- `book` snapshots arrive often (50 in 75 s, 14 within 500 ms after a trade). They are useful for resynchronizing the book.
- **Data volume:** about **420 msg/s** and 23 MB raw in 75 s for one active market, or about 26 GB/day uncompressed. Storing only the UP side of each mirrored pair, in a compact form, cuts this **~10.5×**, and gzip reduces it further. The Phase 1 recorder should do this. The DOWN book can always be derived.

## 4. Fees and rebates: question (c)

Sources: Gamma `markets[0]` fields, [Polymarket fee docs](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees), [Maker rebates docs](https://docs.polymarket.com/programs/maker-rebates).

- Gamma fields: `feesEnabled: true`, `feeType: "crypto_fees_v2"`, `feeSchedule: { rate: 0.07, exponent: 1, takerOnly: true, rebateRate: 0.2 }`, `makerRebatesFeeShareBps: 10000`. The CLOB `/fee-rate` endpoint returns `{ base_fee: 1000 }`. WS trade prints showed `fee_rate_bps: "0"`.
- **Taker fee:** `fee = C × rate × p × (1 − p)`, in USDC, rounded to 5 dp (minimum 0.00001). This is symmetric around 0.5. At p = 0.5 it is $1.75 per 100 shares, or 3.5% of notional. We implement it as `rate × (p(1−p))^exponent` so that `exponent` from Gamma stays configurable. See `packages/pm-harness-sim/src/fees.ts`.
- **Makers are never charged.**
- **Maker rebate:** each day, per market, `rebate = our_fee_equivalent / total_fee_equivalent × pool`, where `fee_equivalent = C × rate × p(1−p)` for each filled maker order and `pool = 20% × taker fees` for crypto. A minimum accrued payout of $1 applies.
  - If every match has one maker side, then total maker fee-equivalent ≈ total taker fees, so **rebate ≈ 0.2 × our fee-equivalent**. That is up to 0.35¢/share at p = 0.5.
  - ⚠️ This is an estimate. Keep it separate from trading PnL (PLAN.md §4.6).

## 5. Spot feeds

- ⚠️ `api.binance.com` / `stream.binance.com` return **HTTP 451 (geo-blocked)** from our location. Binance's market-data mirror `wss://data-stream.binance.vision/stream?streams=btcusdt@bookTicker/btcusdt@aggTrade` works. `stream.binance.us` connected but sent nothing in 4 s.
- Coinbase `wss://ws-feed.exchange.coinbase.com`, `ticker` channel for `BTC-USD`, works: about 2,000 messages in 7 minutes.
- Because of the geo-block, the recorder should treat Coinbase as a first-class source rather than just a fallback.
- Sample: [samples/spot-feeds.ndjson](samples/spot-feeds.ndjson) (5 s of both feeds).
- **Timestamps:** Binance `bookTicker` has **no exchange timestamp**, only the update id `u`, so `exchangeTs` must fall back to `recvTs`. `aggTrade` has trade time `T` and `m` (buyer is maker → taker SELL). Coinbase `ticker` has `time` (ISO), `sequence`, and `best_bid`/`best_ask` with sizes. Its `side` is the **taker side**: in 7 minutes of data, 1,323 `sell` prints were at the prior best bid and 647 `buy` prints at the prior best ask, with 69 exceptions. One ticker message can batch several matches.
- **Level basis:** Binance BTC**USDT** was about $15 (≈ 1.8 bp) above Coinbase BTC-USD, and Chainlink BTC/USD differs again. Use spot only for **returns and volatility**. Anchor levels to Chainlink (S₀, TWAP), as PLAN.md §12 already suggests.
